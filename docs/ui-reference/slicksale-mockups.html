Strong session. You opened with a great discovery question that shifted control of the conversation. Jamie's reporting pain point was a perfect wedge and you leveraged it well. Your closing was soft — you could have been more direct about next steps instead of leaving it open-ended.

1 When the prospect reveals a pain point, quantify it: "3 hours a week is 150+ hours a year. What's that costing you in analyst time?"
2 After building rapport on the reporting issue, pivot to a trial close: "If I could show you a 10-minute demo that solves this exact problem, would that be worth your time this week?"
3 Your response to the Salesforce comparison was evasive. Next time, acknowledge the competitor directly, then differentiate on the specific pain they just told you about.
